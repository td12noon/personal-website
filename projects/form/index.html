Form

My CrossFit training journal. Every class and habit adds a token to the day, the tokens stack, and the stacks are my form.

Today, with the week's stacks up top, and the rare moment it celebrates: a stacked day. Shown with test data.

The problem

Every fitness app I've tried wants me on its program. I already have one. At CrossFit, the day's workout goes up on the board, and plenty of people are following a plan from a coach or a physical therapist. None of the apps let me bring that in.

What I wanted was simple to say: log the class I actually did, see it next to the last time I did it, and watch real progress against a few goals I care about, with the detail one tap away.

Why I built it

So I built my own, around four goals: the 1,000 lb club (back squat, bench press and deadlift combined), 12 classes a month, a better VO₂ max, and lower body fat. Everything in Form points back at one of those four.

Stack the days.

What it does

  • Today shows the class programming with my last result right beside it, suggested loads from my best lifts, and a one-tap "use last weight".
  • Progress turns each lift into a stack of tokens, one per 10 lb, with dashed tokens on top for what's left to my aim.
  • Every goal has a plan next to it: where I stand, what's left, and which class work, side work and habits move it. The plans are plain rules, so I can see exactly why it suggests what it does.
  • Habits, a guided daily mobility routine, and Apple Health imports for the VO₂ and body-fat side.
  • When every daily habit is done, the day is stacked, and a very happy piggy bank takes the tokens.

What it's changed for me

It's new. I built it in the last week of October, so the honest answer is that the jury's still out. The test is simple: more months at 12 classes, and a bigger total.

What I already like is what it rewards. Showing up is the bottom token on every stack, and my form is shown, never scored. No streak shaming, no grades.

It also took three rounds of design directions before the token idea clicked. Now every screen tells the same story, from the week's stacks on Today to the coin you mint when a goal lands.
